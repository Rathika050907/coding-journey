## 🎯 Daily Goal Connection

**How to connect daily tracking with long-term goals:**

1. **Weekly Goal Review:** Every Sunday, review goal progress
2. **Daily Priority Setting:** Each morning, choose 1-2 actions that advance your goals  
3. **Evening Reflection:** Ask "How did today's actions move me toward my goals?"
4. **Monthly Adjustment:** Adjust goals based on what you're learning about yourself
